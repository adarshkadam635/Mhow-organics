THE SMILING WORM — 150 PRODUCT MANUAL-ENTRY VERSION

The existing index.html storefront, CSS, navigation, search, cart, wishlist,
account, pages and footer are preserved.

PRODUCT DATA
------------
All 150 products are now explicit manual slots in app.js inside makeProducts().

Search app.js for:
  PRODUCT 001
  PRODUCT 002
  ...
  PRODUCT 150

For every slot you can manually edit:
- Product name
- Product description
- Price
- Product image path
- Category/subcategory
- Stock and optional metadata

IMAGES
------
Default image slots:
assets/images/products/product-001.svg
...
assets/images/products/product-150.svg

Replace each placeholder with your own JPG, PNG or WebP and update the
image path on that product's `add(...)` line.
